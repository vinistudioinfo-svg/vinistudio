About ViNi Studio

ViNi Studio &
Nikhil Creation

At ViNi Studio, we believe every stage of life deserves to be remembered with elegance, emotion, and authenticity. From the tiny details of a newborn to the joy of childhood, the beauty of motherhood, and the warmth of family, we create timeless memories that tell your unique story.


Founded by Vidya & Nikhil More, ViNi Studio combines creativity, patience, and specialized photography techniques to provide a calm, safe, and personalized experience for every family. Backed by Nikhil Creation's professional photography experience, our goal is to create meaningful memories you and your family will cherish forever.
